# Klara – Product Context

## What it is
Klara is an AI reporting system for the whole construction site. Site managers and foremen talk to Klara by voice in any language (German, Polish, Romanian, Turkish, Russian). Klara writes German daily reports (Bautagesberichte), meeting minutes (Baubesprechungsprotokolle) and VOB/B notices:
- Behinderungsanzeige (§ 6 Abs. 1 VOB/B)
- Mehrkostenanzeige (§ 2 Abs. 5/6 VOB/B)
- Confirmation of verbal orders (Bestätigung mündlicher Anordnungen)

Klara checks completeness, asks follow-up questions in the worker's language, and shows the office what is missing.

## Workflow
1. Polier/Bauleiter speaks to Klara on the phone (voice first, chat with document cards).
2. Klara drafts the German document and checks it for completeness.
3. If something is missing, Klara asks back in the speaker's language, or calls ("Klara ruft an").
4. The office sees each case with a traffic-light status: ready, missing / auto-corrected, blocked.
5. Admins validate or return documents.
6. Klara drafts an escalation email to management about missing items.

## Users
- **Polier / Bauleiter** (site): phone, outdoors, gloves, noise, often non-native German speakers.
- **Office / project lead / management**: desktop, many projects and cases at once.

## Audience for the demo
Jury: German construction professionals aged 40–50. Klara must look trustworthy, calm and modern, not playful.

## Tone
Sachlich, klar, verlässlich. German UI copy, formal "Sie" in documents, short and direct in the app.
